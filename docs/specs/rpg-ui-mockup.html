SKYRIOT · RPG UI mockup

1

Character (P). Paper-doll around the Frame. Left: Armor, Engine, Radar. Right: Primary (STD), Secondary (ADV), Cosmetic. Charm slots below, with real-time countdowns; slot III opens at Level 60. Frame tabs switch the Loadout you are editing. Stats are resolved numbers; the coloured column previews the hovered item.

2

Tooltip. Always the same order: name in rarity colour and +N, kind and tier, stats with a vs Equipped column (sign and colour), Mods (an unchosen Mod waits for the Pilot's pick), a one-word verdict, flavour, sell price. The compared slot is outlined in the Character window.

3

Inventory (I). 8 × 6 bag. Rarity is the cell frame (Standard faint, then Tuned green, Custom blue, Prototype violet, Relic amber). +N top-right, stack count bottom-right, tier as the trim line under the icon.

4

Skill book (K). The Frame's whole Skill Kit with category, cooldown, duration and where it sits on the Hotbar. Here Blind Tag is being dragged out; it isn't on any bar yet.

5

Hotbar. Any Skill or Consumable on any slot of any bar, saved per Pilot per Frame. While dragging, empty slots show dashed targets and the slot under the cursor lights up. "Show all bars" stacks bar 2 (Shift + key) above bar 1. Padlock: lock the bar against accidental drags.

·

Chrome. Gunmetal plate, brass rim, 45° chamfered corners, rivets only at the corners, inset wells for slots. Saira Condensed for titles and captions, Barlow with tabular numbers for everything you read. Skill and weapon icons are the game's own art.
