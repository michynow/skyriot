# Deterministic upgrades and a 10-hour climb to level 100

Product decision against the genre norm: gear Upgrades (+0→+10) never fail or break, and level 1→100 is tuned for 8–10 hours. This removes the grind and the gambling/pay-to-win loop the genre is known for. Future economy features (trading, drops) must not reintroduce RNG failure on upgrades.
